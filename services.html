🛡️ Mobile app management

The engagement that starts after everyone else stops paying attention.

A mobile app is not a deliverable, it is an operating commitment. OS versions move, store rules change, dependencies rot, and a bad release reaches a million people before you notice. Our mobile app management service is the standing arrangement that keeps all of that handled.

We work with publishers, product owners and agencies who already have a live app and need a dependable engineering presence around it — whether that is a full-time-equivalent team, a targeted safety net, or an interim bridge between an in-house hire and a long-term contract.

Four pillars of the managed service

Release management

A planned cadence, an environment that matches production, version discipline, and a rollback path that has actually been rehearsed.

Incident response

Crash triage within hours, root-cause analysis that reaches the actual cause, and a fix that lands as a release rather than a note.

Store operations

Submissions, rejections, listing upkeep, release notes and the metadata work that keeps ranking moving after launch.

Privacy & SDK governance

Every new SDK goes through a privacy review before it ships: what it collects, what its lawful basis is, and how a user opts out.

📊 Advertising & ad-tech

Four formats, one consent decision, real numbers.

We design advertising systems for apps that need to earn without eroding retention. The rule is simple: every format earns its place by being skippable, clearly labelled and legal in every market we ship to.

Advertising formats used in our own apps, and the rules we hold ourselves to
FormatWhere it appears and the rules we hold ourselves to
App-open (splash)Cold-start full screen, close control present from the first frame, capped per session, disabled in child-directed builds.
Rewarded videoAlways opt-in, always skippable, reward granted once per completed view, never partial.
InterstitialNatural break points only, immediate close control, never chained, never during gameplay.
BannerReserved layout area, never overlapping controls, never causing layout shift.

Demand comes from a wide network stack

Demand is aggregated through a wide network stack — Google AdMob, Google Ad Manager, AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay, ironSource, Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Vungle, Chartboost, InMobi, Tapjoy, Mintegral, Liftoff Monetize, Moloco, Digital Turbine, Yahoo, Smaato, Start.io, Appodeal, Amazon Publisher Services, BidMachine, Yandex Ads, HUAWEI Ads, Xiaomi Ads and Kuaishou Ads — with the platform consent signal propagated to every one of them.

  • Google UMP consent on Android, Apple ATT on iOS, IAB TCF and GPC honoured end to end
  • Non-personalised advertising only for children, with no exceptions
  • app-ads.txt configured and maintained
  • Full partner-by-partner data disclosure published in our Privacy Policy

Revenue you can explain

Fill rate, eCPM, ARPDAU, retention and the consent state at request time are reported together, so you can tell whether a revenue drop is a demand problem or a measurement problem.
