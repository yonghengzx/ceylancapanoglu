Monetization

Ads that behave. Revenue that survives a policy review.

Our own apps carry four advertising formats. Each one has a defined trigger, a visible user control and a consent rule — because an ad that annoys people is a revenue bug, not a feature.

App-open (splash)

A full-screen ad at cold start, with an always-visible close control from the first frame and no forced watch time.

User control: Close is tappable immediately; the ad can never trigger an install by itself.

Rewarded video

The player opts in every time, watches to the end, and receives a reward. Close early and nothing is granted.

User control: Never auto-plays; no partial rewards; granted exactly once per completed view.

Interstitial

Full-screen ads at natural breaks — between levels, on leaving a session — never mid-gameplay and never back-to-back.

User control: Close available immediately on appearance, and again after the countdown.

Banner

A small embedded unit inside a reserved layout area. It never overlays a button and never shifts the layout during play.

User control: Opens in an in-app browser, never inside the game screen.

One consent decision, honoured by every network

Demand comes from a broad network stack — Google AdMob and Ad Manager, AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay, ironSource, Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Vungle, Chartboost, InMobi, Tapjoy, Mintegral, Liftoff Monetize, Moloco, Digital Turbine, Yahoo, Smaato, Start.io, Appodeal, Amazon Publisher Services, BidMachine, Yandex Ads, HUAWEI Ads, Xiaomi Ads, Kuaishou Ads and more — all mediated behind a single consent signal.

  • One consent decision, honoured by every network in the chain.
  • Non-personalised ads for children, always, with no exceptions.
  • Consent signals — including Global Privacy Control — are respected, not just stored.
