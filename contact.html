Before you write

What to include in your first message.

You do not need answers for everything. A discovery phase is a normal way to start, and we will tell you if that is the right first step for you.

Product

  • The problem the app solves
  • Who it is for
  • The main screen or core loop

Platform

  • Android, iOS or both
  • The minimum OS version you need to support
  • Any device classes that matter

State

  • What exists today — concept, prototype, build or live app
  • What is blocking you right now

Commercial

  • Budget range
  • Target launch window
  • Who signs off

Store & legal

  • Whether you have store accounts
  • Whether you have a privacy policy already
  • Your target age rating

Links

  • A prototype, a document, a store listing or an existing app we should look at
